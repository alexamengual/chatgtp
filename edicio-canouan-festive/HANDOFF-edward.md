## The open task: an art writing style, and the artist letter

Alex: the draft invitation in the concept doc "sounds extremely corporate and
cold". It needs to "envelop the artist with a sense of talking to someone who
understands them, appreciates them, and wants to help them, and not just some
kind of business offer". He asked us to find the earlier work studying art
critics and writers, finish it into one writing style for all art-related text,
and then rewrite the letter.

What exists in Alex OS:

- **ChatGPT, "Entrevista para crítica artística"** (Dec 2024 – Jul 2025,
  id df510387-6b99-4867-a317-3c69d61d9254).
  - ChatGPT interviewed Alex in the manner of Jerry Saltz and wrote a review,
    then a second review in the manner of Jonathan Jones.
  - It also produced an artist statement (Abstract Expressionism and
    Post-Photography; his word is "profound"), a formal bio, and Mallorcan
    Catalan versions of both.
  - This is the "critics" work. It never became a style guide, and its prose
    is generic ChatGPT ("ingenious paradox", "the real magic lies").
  - The useful material is Alex's own answers:
    - He reduces the visual so the emotional can come through.
    - He compares the print to an ageing face: we stop looking at the beauty
      and fall in love with the character.
    - Viewers recognise something "they already had inside them".
    - His father was his great inspiration.
- **ChatGPT, "Overview and next steps"** (Nov 2025). It claims "a full
  vocabulary for reviews, interviews, and how critics should describe your
  work (Jerry Saltz style)", but no such vocabulary was found in the export.
- **Library, "How to write emails like Alex"**: the only finished style guide.
  - British spelling; no emoji, no bullet points in emails, no em dashes
    (use a spaced hyphen).
  - Never the word "quietly".
  - Warm and direct, and written as a collaborator, never a supplier.
  - Short paragraphs; sign off "Alex".
- **Library, "How we use AI"**: words are written by people; AI drafts, and
  Alex rewrites.
- **Edward's profile**: texts are edited in Alex's voice, never replaced by a
  generic one. Push back on anything that sounds like marketing. Maxwell
  Perkins is a model.
- **amengual.com/about**, the published voice:
  - "surface beauty is the least of what an image can hold"
  - photography "not an end but an instrument"
  - "island life from an islander's point of view"
