# Caribbean art: the master file

One place for everything known about Caribbean art for the Edicio × Canouan
Festive limited edition. It brings together Scott's September research, the *A
to Z of Caribbean Art* and the Mustique art show. Last updated 17 Oct 2026.

## The decisions that frame this (from Alex OS)

- **Quality first, pan-Caribbean** (Alex, 25 Sep): "great art, not locals who
  play with paint". This overrides Scott's first recommendation of a
  Vincentian-only room.
- **Relationships are personal, never the Mustique Company.** The company sees
  Canouan as a competitor. Alex knows **Tiqui Atencio Demirdjian** (Mustique
  show founder) and **Anjeni Ramtahal** (its curator, LES ÎLES gallery,
  Amsterdam). Anjeni's own experience with Mustique was not a good one, and
  she is the natural curator or partner.
- **Alex is always "artist" (or "visual artist"), never "photographer"** (Alex,
  18 Oct). His bio (amengual.com/about): photography is "not an end but an
  instrument" in a practice built on screen printing and the physical matter of
  place; "photographer" makes people think of weddings and campaigns.
- **Alex exhibits as one of the 12, as host artist** (Alex, 18 Oct): a peer
  show where a local artist invites colleagues, on the same terms as everyone
  else. This overrides Scott's September advice to keep Alex's own work out of
  the room; the reasoning is that a commercial creative role reads as genuine
  only if he is established as a working artist from the start.
- **Round one** (Alex, 18 Oct): five photographers, six in other media, and
  Alex; no LES ÎLES artists; no one who has shown or will show at Mustique;
  no artist travel unless self-funded; estate cost limited to the room and the
  display prints; 10% of sales to Canouan. See `shortlist.md`.
- **Art is outside the Canouan contract** (clause 2.3), so it's pitched as its
  own project.
- **Related threads:** the Canouan artist edition (20 copies with a signed
  print, in the agenda's research queue); the MOCAN corridor art audit with
  Jonathan Payne, which could fund an in-house large-format printer (14 Oct);
  the edicio.art shop.

### 3. The *A to Z of Caribbean Art* (established, from the early 20th century to now)

266 artists from the English-, Spanish-, French- and Dutch-speaking Caribbean
and the diaspora. After two research passes (221 of 266 researched): **127
living**, 82 deceased, 57 unknown; 57 artist websites and 8 Instagram handles
found. By country: Cuba 43, Jamaica 34, Haiti 29, Trinidad & Tobago 27,
Puerto Rico 18, Dominican Republic 16, Guyana 11, The Bahamas 8, Barbados 6+,
the French and Dutch Caribbean and Suriname about 20. The **Eastern
Caribbean is thin**: one Vincentian entry each for Huggins and Bynoe, Canute
Caliste (Carriacou, deceased) and Llewellyn Xavier (Saint Lucia).
See `az-artists.md`.
