# Potential advisors: the A to Z of Caribbean Art team

The people behind *A to Z of Caribbean Art* (Robert & Christopher Publishers,
Port of Spain, November 2019; 304 pages; ISBN 978-976-95344-9-0). They are a
ready-made, region-by-region panel of Caribbean art expertise and potential
advisors for EDICIÓ × Canouan.

Source: the book's product page,
https://robertandchristopher.com/product/a-to-z-of-caribbean-art/ (read via
search on 17 Oct 2026; the site itself is blocked from the research
environment).

Status key: **checked** = current role confirmed in a 2024–26 source ·
**dated** = role found, but the source may be old · **to check** = not yet
researched (the search budget ran out).

## First read: who to talk to first

1. **Holly Bynoe**: Vincentian, with Bequia ties, a co-founder of ARC with
   Nadia Huggins, and already in Alex's network. Scott's research had her as
   first-choice co-curator before Alex's 25 Sep correction moved Anjeni
   Ramtahal ahead. As an advisor she bridges both.
2. **Annalee Davis**: runs the nearest residency infrastructure (Fresh Milk,
   Barbados) and the regional networks (Caribbean Linked, Tilting Axis).
3. **Veerle Poupeye**: the region's leading art historian; one call gives a
   view of the whole field.
4. **Marina Reyes Franco**: the strongest critical voice on art and tourism.
   Having her view early protects the project against the art-washing reading.
5. **Melanie Archer and Mariel Brown**: publishing peers. A possible co-imprint
   or launch-text partner rather than advisors.

Not yet considered: Anjeni Ramtahal and Tiqui Atencio Demirdjian (Mustique),
who aren't part of the book but are Alex's own contacts. See `master.md`.
