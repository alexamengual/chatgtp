## Research questions (for the independent research step)

- Edition format: print, object, textile, ceramic? Run size, price point, and
  what a festive-season guest at Canouan actually takes home
- Representation: does SVG and the Grenadines have a place in the list, not
  only the larger Caribbean art scenes?
- Precedents: limited editions launched by Caribbean resorts or festivals, and
  how they credited and paid artists
- Rights and terms: edition size, artist fee or royalty, signing, certificates
- Logistics: production lead time against the festive dates
