## Flags on the rated artists

- **Big gallery:** Chris Ofili (Absolutely), Kelly Sinnapah Mary (Maybe).
  Approach Ofili personally as a stretch; plan the 12 without him.
- **Mustique show, tread lightly:** Akilah Watts (Yes), Ronald Williams (Maybe).
- **Hard to print:** Dubréus Lhérisson (Yes) makes sequined Vodou flags and
  sculpture. A photograph of a flag would work as a print, but it's a step
  further from the original than a photograph or print is.
- **Few photographers:** only 5 of the 34 Maybe-or-better are photographers
  (Nadia Huggins, Richard Mark Rawlins, Juan Carlos Alom, René Peña, Terry
  Boddie). The pool has about 27 more unrated photographers, worth one quick
  pass with the Photographers filter, given the residency plan.
- **Logistics:** 7 of the 34 live in Haiti and 3 in Cuba. Payments and courier
  timings there are slower, so keep them to a few in round one. Barbados (5) is
  the easiest: direct flights to Canouan (Alex's note on Ras Ishi Butcher).
