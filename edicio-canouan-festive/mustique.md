# The Mustique Caribbean Contemporary Art Show & Prize

Research of 17 Oct 2026. mustiqueart.com and Instagram are blocked from the
research environment, so everything here comes from search snippets of the
official site and the press. Each claim carries its source.

## What changes from Scott's September research

1. **The three Vincentians are real and verified.** Maurel Reynolds,
   Romario Martindale (Bequia) and Zen Punnett were on the **2024** longlist,
   which is on the official site. Scott's file had them as unconfirmed leads.
2. **No 2026 longlist has been published that we can find.** The "16-name 2026
   longlist with no Vincentian" in Scott's file was most likely the 2024

   "Caribbean Soul" was the **2024** theme.
3. **The 2026 dates are not announced.** The site says only "December 2026,
   please stay tuned". The "26 Dec – 5 Jan" in Scott's file are the dates on
   the site's 2024 section, carried over a year. Confirm with Anjeni before
   planning around them.
4. **The 2026 nominators** (Akilah Watts, Christopher Cozier, Dale Ramirez,
   Tiqui) could not be confirmed in any source.

## The show

- **What:** a charitable biennial for emerging artists who live in the
  Caribbean and have **never had representation outside the region**. Entry
  is by invitation only: a nominating committee covering the English-, French-,
  Spanish- and Dutch-speaking Caribbean proposes artists, a longlist jury picks
  about 25 works, and a final jury picks three winners.
  ([about](https://mustiqueart.com/pages/about))
- **Founders and sponsors:** Tiqui Atencio Demirdjian and Ago Demirdjian.
  Supporters: The Mustique Company, Air Adelphi, Caribbean Airlines, Print All
  Over Me, Drukkerij Printvisie, Whispering Angel rosé.
- **Organiser:** "Caribbean Art Promoter & Curator, Anjeni Ramtahal"
  ([sponsors page](https://mustiqueart.com/pages/sponsors)). The about page
  credits "Organizational Support: Zoe Jenner".
- **Money:** a total shared prize of US$20,000. All works are for sale, with
  50% of proceeds to the Mustique Educational Trust
  ([The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2024/12/24/taking-the-mick-rolling-stones-singer-jagger-is-a-judge-on-caribbean-art-jury)).

### 2024, first edition

- Mustique Community Centre; prize night **28 Dec 2024**. Exhibition dates
  differ: 27–29 Dec (The Art Newspaper) or 26 Dec 2024 – 5 Jan 2025 (site).
- Theme: **"Caribbean Soul"**.
- Longlist jury: Dr Marsha Pearce (also an A to Z advisor), Simon de Pury,
  Amanda Coulson, James Oscar, Lucia Pizzani, Tobias Ostrander (Tate), Ana
  María Celis (Christie's).
- Final jury: Mick Jagger, Marc Quinn, Maja Hoffmann, Tiqui and Ago
  Demirdjian, Anjeni Ramtahal "and others".
- Winners: **1st Akilah Watts** (Barbados), *Heart*; **2nd Brian Ashing**
  (Trinidad & Tobago), *Paradise of Scorched Earth*, which Jagger bought;
  **3rd Anna Gibson** (Barbados), *Festering Fantasy: Celebrate*
  ([Loop, 7 Jan 2025](https://www.loopnews.com/content/rock-legend-mick-jagger-purchases-brian-ashing-painting/)).

### 2026, second edition

- "December 2026": dates, venue, theme and prize are not announced in any
  source found.
- Final jury ([site](https://mustiqueart.com/pages/shortlist-winner-jury)):
  Ago Demirdjian, Basil Charles, David Ross, Ebs Burnough, Emma Pilkington,
  Farida Khelfa, Maja Hoffmann, Marc Quinn, Paul Henkel, Peter Simon, Pierre
  Lagrange, Sophie Main, Sylvie Winkler, Vera Michalski-Hoffmann, plus "a very
  special celebrity guest".
- Some artist pages carry a header reading "2026 Longlisted artworks for the
  2nd Edition", but the artists shown are 2024 longlisters. It's probably a
  shared template, not a new list.

## What it means for the Edicio edition

- The Mustique artists are, by rule, **unrepresented emerging artists**. An
  Edicio edition is a real step up for them (a published, signed edition), and
  Anjeni already knows them all. They are the most natural first invitations
  through her.
- **The three Vincentians** fit Scott's original "country seen from inside"
  idea, and Martindale is on Bequia, next door. Since Alex's 25 Sep correction
  ("quality first"), they belong on the list on merit, so see their work
  before deciding.
- Keep it personal with Tiqui and Anjeni and never through the Mustique
  Company (Alex, 25 Sep).
